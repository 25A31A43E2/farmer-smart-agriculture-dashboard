# 🌱 SmartAgri – Farmer-Friendly Smart Agriculture Dashboard

**SmartAgri** is a farmer-friendly web-based smart agriculture dashboard designed to bring important farming information into one simple platform.

It helps farmers understand **weather, soil, irrigation, crops, and farm-related financial information** through a simple and easy-to-use interface.

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Farmers often need information from different sources to make daily farming decisions. Weather conditions, soil health, irrigation requirements, crop conditions, and financial information can be difficult to manage together.

SmartAgri aims to provide these important details in **one centralized dashboard**.

## 💡 Our Solution

SmartAgri provides a simple dashboard where farmers can access:

* 🌦️ Weather Information
* 🌱 Soil Information
* 💧 Irrigation Information
* 🌾 Crop Information
* 💰 Wealth Card
* 🚨 Important Alerts

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌦️ Weather

Provides useful weather information that can help farmers plan farming activities.

### 🌱 Soil

Displays important soil-related information such as:

* Soil moisture
* pH
* Nutrient information

### 💧 Irrigation

Helps farmers understand irrigation requirements using available soil and weather information.

### 🌾 Crops

Provides crop-related information and helps monitor crop health and possible alerts.

### 💰 Wealth Card

Provides a simple view of farming-related financial information such as income and expenses.

### 🚨 Alerts

Highlights important information that may require the farmer's attention.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Make agricultural information easier to understand.
* Bring important farm information into one platform.
* Support better day-to-day farming decisions.
* Reduce unnecessary water usage through better irrigation awareness.
* Make technology more accessible to farmers.
